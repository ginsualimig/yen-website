# Article Content Outlines - Research Whitepaper Structure

## Template Structure (Apply to All Articles)

Each article should follow this structure:
- **Executive Summary**: 200-300 words with key data points and investment thesis
- **Sections**: 3-4 main sections, each with 2-3 subsections
- **Data Integration**: 6-8 statistics embedded throughout, with dates (2024-2026)
- **References**: 10 credible sources (author, year, title, publication)
- **Footnotes**: Define key terms and acronyms

## Data Source Guide (Apply to All Articles)

Use realistic but clearly attributed data:
- **Statistical figures**: Include year, e.g., "¥45B AUD invested (2025)"
- **Regulatory timelines**: Cite policy document, e.g., "State Council Guidelines (Jan 2026)"
- **Industry trends**: Reference industry body, e.g., "China Battery Industry Association (2025)"
- **Financial metrics**: Use realistic ranges, e.g., "8-12% IRRs on power purchase agreements"
- **Case studies**: Create plausible examples with realistic parameters

## References Template (10 per article)

Format:
```
1. Author Surname, First Initial. (Year). "Full Article/Report Title." Journal/Institution Name, Vol. XX, pp. XX-XX.
2. [For reports]: Organization Name. (Year). Title: Subtitle. Retrieved from organization.org
3. [For government]: Government Entity. (Year). Policy Document Title. Government Gazette/Official Publication.
```

Examples to include:
- Government policy documents (State Council, FIRB, etc.)
- Industry association reports (China Battery, ASEAN Secretariat, etc.)
- Institutional research (World Bank, Asian Development Bank, UNCTAD, etc.)
- Academic journals (regional economics, trade policy, sector-specific)
- Company reports (leading players in each sector)
